# FOUNDERS CHEAT SHEET

**A pattern is the move behind the move.** (Tactic = "got rail rebates." Pattern = "own the boring thing everyone needs, set the rules for both sides.")

## The 6 that keep repeating
1. **Own the boring link** everyone depends on — Rockefeller, Kroc
2. **Standardize a chaotic industry** from the top — McDonald's, Ford
3. **Buy what the crowd priced at zero** — Walton's small towns, Buffett
4. **Lock a thing + its recurring add-on** — Polaroid, Apple
5. **Refuse the fight everyone calls "the only game"** — Jobs, DHH
6. **Beware leverage creep** — borrowed expansion killed Insull

## Seeing a new pattern
Don't guess — **trace it.** Follow the money, find the boring link, ask "seen this shape before?" If something works that "shouldn't," the **3rd time** across different markets = a new pattern. Date it.

## What it's for
- **Money:** shows where profit *has to* flow next — get in before the crowd.
- **Direction:** whoever holds the boring-layer leverage wins.
- **Think different:** the crowd has the same facts — winners pick which one is load-bearing. (Everyone saw railroads as expense; Rockefeller saw them as the game.)

## The psychology
- **Focus** = one boring thing, refused distractions.
- **Problems** = re-aim, don't stay positive ("nothing wrong with my aim, just gotta change the target").
- **Family** = they ran on a backstop; those who *avoided* the family cost did just as well.
- **Discontent** = the engine, but useless alone — only fuel for re-aiming.

## One line
Everyone sees the same facts — the edge is choosing which boring fact is the whole game, staying on it, never settling at "good enough."
